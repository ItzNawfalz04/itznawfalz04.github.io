About Me

Hi, my name is Nawfal 👋…

I am currently a university student studying Software Engineering at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM).

My journey in Computer Science began when I enrolled in the Diploma in Computer Science at UTM. Unlike many who started coding from a young age, I only discovered what is Computer Science after finishing SPM (Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia). I have always enjoyed Mathematics (even if I am not the best at it 😄), problem-solving, and working with computers. So, choosing this field felt natural for me.

I previously completed my Industrial Training as an Information Technology Intern at Felda Travel Sdn. Bhd., a travel management company under FGV Holdings Berhad. The experience gave me valuable hands-on exposure to real workplace environments, and I am eager to explore more opportunities to further sharpen my skills and gain industry exposure.

In my free time, I will working be on personal projects completing my university assigments, learning new things, and improving my existing skills. When I am not doing any of those, you will find me watching movies, browsing internet, keeping my living space clean and tidy, or taking evening walks to relax.
