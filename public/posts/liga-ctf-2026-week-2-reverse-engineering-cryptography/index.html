Introduction 👋

LIGA CTF 2026 is a six-weekend, category-focused Capture The Flag (CTF) competition running from May to July 2026 organised by OWASP Malaysia Federation (Kuala Lumpur Chapter) . Each weekend focuses on a specific cybersecurity discipline, allowing participants to spend more time exploring a particular domain instead of handling multiple categories simultaneously.

I participated in this competition in Student Category together with two other teammates. This write-up documents the challenges that I successfully solved throughout the competition.

Week 2 focused on Boot-to-Root, running from 29 May 2026, 08:00 PM until 31 May 2026, 11:59 PM. During this phase, I successfully solved 4 challenges.

✏️ Note

This is my first time writing a proper CTF write-up. I am still improving my technical writing style from time to time.
