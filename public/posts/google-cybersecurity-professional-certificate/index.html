Portfolio Activity: Draft a Professional Statement

This portfolio activity focused on crafting a concise and authentic professional introduction that communicates who I am as an aspiring cybersecurity professional. The goal was to move beyond a simple job title and create a compelling narrative that highlights my core strengths, personal values, and genuine passion for the field. By reflecting on my background as a Software Engineering student at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) and my commitment to continuous learning, I developed a statement that bridges my technical roots in software development with my growing interest in cybersecurity.

Drafted Professional Statement:

As a Software Engineering student at UTM with a passion for building reliable technology and a commitment to continuous learning, I am driven to bridge the gap between software development and cybersecurity. My strengths in system architecture, problem-solving, and clear documentation enable me to identify vulnerabilities and integrate secure coding practices early in the development lifecycle. I aim to help organizations proactively protect their digital assets by ensuring security is embedded from the very first line of code.

Portfolio Activity: Conduct a Security Audit

This portfolio activity involved conducting a comprehensive internal security audit for Botium Toys, a fictional small U.S. toy retailer expanding its online presence to international markets. The audit was initiated to address growing concerns about maintaining compliance and securing critical assets amidst business growth. My task was to review the company’s existing scope, goals, and risk assessment provided by the IT department and then perform a detailed controls and compliance assessment using the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF).
